# Declaration of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies in the Writing Process

During the preparation of this work, the author used Anthropic's Claude in
two capacities: (1) as an AI-assisted language tool for grammar correction,
copy-editing, and formatting of the manuscript text; and (2) as an
AI-assisted coding aid (Claude Code) to support the development,
refactoring, and documentation of the analysis and experimentation software
underlying the reported results. After using these tools, the author
reviewed, verified, and edited all content and code as needed, and takes
full responsibility for the integrity, accuracy, and originality of the
published work.
