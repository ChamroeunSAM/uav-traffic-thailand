                                       the trajectory-quality framework)
  DOTTED RECTANGLE (top, full width) . the ground-truth feedback bar
  Bold serif title in each box. Black outlines / white fills; light-grey fill
  ONLY for the accent group (Trajectory-quality) and the GT-free branch.

LAYOUT — a snake (same as the reference)
----------------------------------------
  TOP ROW  L->R:  Input -> Data prep -> Dataset -> Detector benchmark
  turn down on the right, then
  BOTTOM ROW R->L: Video GT -> Tracker benchmark -> Trajectory-quality -> Results
  ACROSS THE TOP (spanning the top row, arrows dropping down): Ground-truth bar.
  One BRANCH drops off Trajectory-quality: the GT-free path.
